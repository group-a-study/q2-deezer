Deezer on Q2

Sign the Q2 in to your Deezer account.

Enter the PIN on the Q2's screen

On the Q2, open Streaming → Deezer and pick what to download. You can close this page.

1. Copy your ARL from a computer

The ARL is the sign-in cookie deezer.com keeps. The Q2 has no browser, so it borrows yours.

  1. On a computer, sign in at deezer.com.
  2. Open the developer tools: F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac.
  3. Chrome / Edge: Application → Cookies → https://www.deezer.com.
    Firefox: Storage → Cookies → https://www.deezer.com.
    Safari: Storage → Cookies (turn on Develop in Settings → Advanced first).
  4. Copy the Value of the cookie named arl: a long run of letters and digits.

2. Paste it here

It's checked with Deezer, then saved on the Q2's card in .deezer/arl. Treat it like a password: anyone with it can use your account. A paid Deezer plan is needed: songs download to the Q2's card in FLAC or MP3, then play from it.
